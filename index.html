About Me

About My Work

I am passionate about transforming spaces through interior design. I like to think of each space as a combination of light, materials, and the way people truly live in and connect with their surroundings.

I have a special interest in residential architecture, especially apartments. I enjoy working with neutral and dark color palettes, natural materials, and design solutions that balance aesthetics, functionality, and comfort. I have been creating interior design projects since 2023. It all started during school breaks, when I would draw floor plans and room sketches as a hobby. Through this process, I discovered my passion for interior design. Since then, I have been exploring different styles, developing my skills, and turning ideas into projects visualized in RoomPlanner. The software allows me to develop and visualize each space, from the initial concepts to the final design proposal.
